Google Drive Direct Link Generator

Convert supported Google Drive sharing links to download-style links

Example

Input: https://drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID/view?usp=drive_link
Output: https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=FILE_ID

Limitations

This pattern is intended for uploaded files whose sharing settings allow downloading. Google Drive may still show access, confirmation, quota, or virus-scan pages. Native Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides require export URLs and are not supported by this converter.
